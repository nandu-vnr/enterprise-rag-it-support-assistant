# P1 Incident Escalation Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines the escalation path for P1 incidents affecting IT support, deployments, or production systems.

## Steps
1. Confirm incident severity and impact.
2. Notify the incident response team and on-call support.
3. Document the incident in the ticketing system.
4. Escalate to senior engineering and operations if the issue cannot be resolved within 30 minutes.

## Communication
Keep stakeholders updated on status and expected recovery time.
