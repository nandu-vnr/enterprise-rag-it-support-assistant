# Access Request Policy

## Purpose
This policy governs how access requests are approved for IT systems, applications, and infrastructure.

## Process
1. Validate the requester identity and business justification.
2. Confirm least-privilege access is being requested.
3. Approve access with a change ticket and owner sign-off.
4. Review access after the request is fulfilled.

## Notes
Access changes must be logged and tracked in the ticketing system.
